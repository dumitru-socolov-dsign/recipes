The side that goes with any meat in the plan and cooks in parallel without needing your
attention.

**Why frozen and not fresh.** Half the price, already cut, they don't spoil in the fridge if the
session slips a day, and — unlike the fresh ones — they were frozen within hours of harvest, so
they lose nothing. The only drawback is water, and water is solved with a hot oven and plenty of
fat.

**Single layer, no stirring.** Those are the two rules. Crowded pieces release water and steam;
pieces stirred halfway lose the crust they were busy building. Resist the urge to open the oven.

**The walnuts do what the sweetness of root vegetables used to do.** Cauliflower and broccoli
never caramelise the way carrot or parsnip does — they don't have the sugar for it. What they're
missing isn't sweetness, it's contrast: something crunchy and fatty on top. Forty grams of
toasted walnut fixes that and adds 260 calories on the way.
