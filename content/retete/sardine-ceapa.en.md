The plan has a hole in it: by Friday lunchtime Tuesday's food has run out and the cooking
session isn't until the evening. This plugs it without turning anything on.

**Why sardines and not tuna.** Sardines have four times the omega-3, cost the same, and bring
calcium from the small bones you eat with them. Tuna is lean protein and not much else.

**The calories come from oil and walnuts, not from bulk.** Eighty millilitres of olive oil looks
like a lot poured over a salad. It's over 700 calories and it's the entire point of the recipe —
without it this would be a quarter of a lunch. The cabbage is there for bulk and fibre, not
energy.

**An honest warning:** sardines are high in phosphorus — those same small bones — and so are
walnuts. Together, this is the most phosphorus-heavy meal of the week. Once a week is fine;
don't run it two days in a row.
