The dish that looks after itself. You start it, put the lid on and go do something else.

**Paprika goes in off the heat.** It's the most common mistake in Romanian home cooking:
paprika thrown into boiling fat burns in under ten seconds and turns the whole pot bitter.
Take the pot off the ring, stir the paprika into the still-hot fat, put it back. Simple, and
irreversible if you get it wrong.

**Red peppers, not green.** Red ones are ripe, so they're sweet, and that sweetness makes up
for the missing salt. Green peppers are unripe and slightly bitter — the exact opposite of
what you want here.

**Cream binds the sauce instead of starch.** The potato in the classic version broke down a
little and thickened the liquid. Without it, the sauce stays thin no matter how long you
simmer. Fifty millilitres of double cream, off the heat, does the same job.

**Pork belly brings both the meat and the cooking fat.** Three hundred and twenty grams leave
behind all the lard this recipe needs, so not a gram of added fat goes in at the start. With
the shoulder alongside, that's 470 g of meat for two portions without raising the day's
protein — belly has half the protein of shoulder at twice the calories.

**The next day it's better.** The fat settles, the paprika gives up all its colour and the
peppers go completely soft. This is the dish in the plan that gains the most from a night in
the fridge.
