The fastest dish in the plan and the only one done start to finish on the hob, no oven.

**The mushrooms are the trick.** Browned properly, they produce natural glutamate — the same
compound that makes salt and meat taste more intense. A pan of brown mushrooms does more for
flavour than two grams of salt.

**Double cream at 48%, not single.** It's half the recipe: 240 ml bring over 1100 calories with
only 5 g of protein. Single cream at 20% would bring less than half that and would need twice
the volume to make the same sauce. Being fattier, it also splits far less easily.

**Mustard, not salt.** Ten grams of Dijon bring acidity, bitterness and a little sodium exactly
where you taste them: in the sauce. The same amount of sodium spread through the whole dish
would do nothing.
