The plan's fallback side: under a euro, eight minutes, and it goes with absolutely anything on
the list.

**Cabbage is the underrated vegetable in this diet.** It soaks up fat like a sponge, it's low in
potassium compared with almost any other vegetable, it keeps a fortnight in the fridge without
spoiling, and it costs a euro a head. When you want bulk on the plate with no carbohydrate and
no money, it's the answer.

**Lard for frying, butter at the end.** Lard takes high heat without burning, and cabbage needs
high heat to brown rather than steam. Butter added at the end stays butter: you taste it, and it
takes the side up to 300 calories a portion.

**Caraway, not cumin.** Two different plants, and Romanian mixes them up constantly. Caraway —
the aniseed-tasting seed that belongs with cabbage — is the one here. Cumin, the one from Middle
Eastern cooking, belongs with minced meat, not here.
